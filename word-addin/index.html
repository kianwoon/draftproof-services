Highlight text in Word and scan it for AI‑writing risk — billed to your DraftProof credits.

1 Get your API key

The add‑in signs in with a personal key. Generate one (copy it — it's shown once), then paste it into the add‑in's pane.

2 Add the add‑in to Word

Download the manifest, then follow the steps for your version of Word.

Download manifest.xml

  1. Open a document at word.cloud.microsoft.
  2. Go to Home → Add‑ins → More Add‑ins.
  3. Open the My Add‑ins tab → Upload My Add‑in.
  4. Choose the manifest.xml you downloaded → Upload.
  5. Open it from the Home tab: DraftProof → Scan selection.
  1. Put manifest.xml in a folder and share that folder on your network.
  2. In Word: File → Options → Trust Center → Trust Center Settings → Trusted Add‑in Catalogs.
  3. Paste the folder's share path, click Add catalog, tick Show in Menu, OK, and restart Word.
  4. Insert → My Add‑ins → Shared Folder → select DraftProof.
