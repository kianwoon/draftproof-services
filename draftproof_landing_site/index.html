Content-aware rewrite engine

One rewrite strategy does not fit every document.

DraftProof identifies the writing type first, then chooses the review path that preserves what matters: citations, technical meaning, structure, author context, or genre intent.

Broad explanatory essay

Author-thesis rewrite

Keeps the main argument visible while strengthening the author’s reasoning.

Academic cited text

Citation-preserving rewrite

Protects source claims, citation placement, and evidence relationships.

Technical content

Technical-preserving rewrite

Maintains definitions, constraints, methods, and terminology.

Legal, policy, or medical

Minimal targeted rewrite

Uses conservative edits where over-rewriting could change meaning or risk.

Structured list or table

Structure-preserving rewrite

Keeps rows, bullets, findings, and comparisons usable.

Short text

Expansion or more context

Expands only when there is enough signal; otherwise asks for context.

Personal reflection

Author-grounded rewrite

Preserves lived context, personal framing, and voice.

Creative marketing

Genre-specific rewrite

Matches the intent, audience, and format without forcing academic tone.

Content-aware rewriting, not generic humanizing. DraftProof avoids shallow tactics like synonym swapping, random sentence breaks, or rewriting low-risk text just to chase a score.
